This file exists so a fresh Claude Code session behaves like one that has been here all along. --- ## The commercial situation We are an agency bidding for Almed Retail's Website Rebuild Programme: four F&B brand websites (JJ Chicken, Juan Valdez MENA, Derwandi, Solidaire). Six other agencies have submitted proposals. We are building working sites rather than slide decks, because none of the six showed a single piece of design. **The competing bids, for context on where we sit:** | Agency | Platform | Option 1 price | Support | |---|---|---|---| | TransformUs | WordPress ×4 | ~AED 47k (no content/SEO) | 6 months | | 8cho | undecided, deliberately | AED 62,500 | 30 days | | LDT / Ladera | WordPress + WPML | AED 148,000 | 30 days | | Hovi | WordPress + Woo | ~AED 191,000 | 90 days | | Exquitech | Next.js + shared Strapi | AED 235–250,000 | 15 days | | GLMA | Next.js + custom CMS | AED 350,000 (+72k/yr) | 60 days | Our target: **AED 145,000–165,000** for all four, 90-day support. **Why Almed is sensitive about ownership.** Their previous agency is withholding hosting, CMS and domain credentials in an active dispute. Section 1.1 of their own scope leads with this. Any account we create is created in Almed's name, under Almed's billing, with credentials handed over at kickoff. This is the emotional centre of the whole brief — do not treat it as boilerplate. --- ## About the brand (from Almed's own documents) - Concept created **2010**. Simplicity is a stated operational advantage, not an accident. - Chicken is Grade A, hormone free, grain fed and halal. The logo frames it in a semi-square representing a chicken with beak and crest. - Parent: **Almed Retail**, founded 2015 by Founder and Chairman Mr. Ahmad Zaabri. - Delivery ratings: Deliveroo 4.7, InstaShop 4.7, Talabat 4.5, Careem 4.4, Noon Food 4.4, Google 4.4, ChatFood 4.2. - Social: `@jjchicken`. Ordering: `order.jjchicken.com` (ChatFood). - Franchise contact in the deck: `info@jjchicken.com`, +971 4 320 3930. **Store formats:** stand-alone street shops, mall food courts, and fast-casual mall units. Plus one food truck. --- ## Settled decisions **No prices on the website.** Client instruction. Prices live on ChatFood. `menu.json` carries no price field. **Ordering is outsourced.** Almed is appointing an order-management company. The site links out; it does not transact. An earlier concept included a dormant native ordering module behind a feature flag — that has been dropped. **Homepage carries no menu, locations or franchise content.** Those are second-layer pages. Client instruction. The homepage's job is brand, signature items, and routing. **Al Shamkha removed, Silicon Central DSO added.** Client instruction. Note for the record: Al Shamkha is a live Talabat-exclusive location, so delisting it entirely has a local-search cost. Raised, decision pending. **Sidebar drawer as primary navigation.** Client preference, and it suits the app-style direction the scope asks for. **Static, no framework.** Four HTML pages, one stylesheet, one script, two JSON files. Chosen so the eventual CMS binding is a data-layer swap rather than a rewrite, and so nothing rots. --- ## Things that have burned us before - **The brand book back cover lists only 9 branches.** It is out of date. The Physical Locations spreadsheet is authoritative. Sources disagree on branch count — always use the register. - **The brand book PDF's Arabic extracts corrupted.** Arabic in this repo was written fresh, not lifted from the PDF. It needs native review. - **`order.jjchicken.com` is a ChatFood JS webview.** Its menu cannot be scraped. Menu data was reconstructed from the brand book and business profile. - **A `.ps` file was supplied first and could not be opened.** Always ask for PDF. --- ## Voice Plain, confident, unfussy. Short sentences. The brand's own language is direct — "Where Healthy Meets Tasty", "hormone free, grain fed" — so the copy should be too. No hospitality-brochure adjectives. No "nestled", no "culinary journey", no "tantalising". State what it is and let the fire do the work. Arabic is written for the Gulf market, not translated from English. It should read as though it were drafted in Arabic first.
